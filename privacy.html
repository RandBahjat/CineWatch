1. Overview

Welcome to CineWatch ("we", "us", or "our"). CineWatch is a free movie and TV show browsing, rating, and tracking platform available at cinewatch.watch.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our website and services. By using CineWatch, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Our commitment to you

We only collect the minimum data necessary to provide our service. We do not sell your personal information to anyone, ever.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only what's needed to run CineWatch. Here's exactly what we store:

Account Information (if you sign up)

Name, email address, username, and avatar. This is required to create and manage your CineWatch account.

Usage Data

Your favorites list, watchlist, ratings you give to titles, and your "continue watching" progress. This data is stored in your account so it's available across devices.

Comments

If you post a comment on a title, your comment content, username, and avatar are stored and publicly visible.

Analytics (Anonymous)

We use Google Analytics to understand how people use the site — page views, session duration, and device types. This data is anonymous and cannot identify you personally.

We do NOT collect payment information, phone numbers, physical addresses, or any government ID.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to operate and improve CineWatch:

  • Providing your personalized watchlist, favorites, and progress tracking
  • Displaying your profile, username, and avatar across the site
  • Enabling you to post and manage comments on titles
  • Sending password reset emails when you request them
  • Improving the site experience based on anonymous usage patterns
  • Protecting the site from bots and malicious traffic (via Cloudflare)
We never sell your data

Your personal information is never sold, rented, or traded to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Account & Security

Your account security is handled by Supabase, a trusted authentication platform used by thousands of production applications worldwide.

  • Passwords are bcrypt hashed — even we cannot see your plain-text password
  • Login sessions are managed with JWT tokens that expire automatically
  • All data is protected by Row Level Security (RLS) — users can only access their own data
  • All communication between your browser and our servers is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
  • Password reset links are delivered securely via email and expire after one use
Your account data is isolated

No other user or third party can access your favorites, ratings, or personal information through our system.

5. Third-Party Services

CineWatch relies on the following trusted third-party services:

  • Supabase — Database, authentication, and user data storage. Privacy policy at supabase.com/privacy
  • Cloudflare — CDN, DDoS protection, and the human verification (Turnstile) on the site entry. Privacy policy at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy
  • Google Analytics — Anonymous website usage analytics. You can opt out at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
  • Google Fonts — Fonts are loaded from Google's servers. Their privacy policy applies to font requests
  • TMDB — Movie poster and backdrop images are served from The Movie Database. We do not share user data with them

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them independently.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

CineWatch uses browser storage to provide a better experience:

  • Authentication session — Keeps you logged in between visits (stored via Supabase)
  • Preferences — Language settings and UI preferences stored locally in your browser
  • Security verification — Cloudflare Turnstile uses cookies to verify you are human
  • Analytics — Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish unique visitors (anonymous)

You can clear cookies and local storage at any time through your browser settings. Doing so will log you out and reset any saved preferences.

7. Your Rights

You have full control over your personal data on CineWatch:

  • Access — You can view all data stored in your profile at any time from your account page
  • Edit — You can update your name, username, and avatar from your profile settings
  • Delete comments — You can delete any comment you've posted from the title page
  • Delete your account — Contact us and we will permanently delete your account and all associated data within 30 days
  • Data export — Contact us to request an export of your personal data

8. Children's Privacy

CineWatch is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our service evolves. When we make significant changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of CineWatch after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
