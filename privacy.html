1. Overview

Welcome to CineWatch ("we", "us", or "our"). CineWatch is a free movie and TV show browsing, rating, and tracking platform available at cinewatch.watch.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our website and services. By using CineWatch, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Our commitment to you

We only collect the minimum data necessary to provide our service. We do not sell your personal information to anyone, ever.

2. Data We Collect

2. Data We Collect

We collect only what is strictly required to provide a seamless streaming and tracking experience. Here is an exhaustive breakdown of what we store:

Account Information (Optional Registration)

Name, email address, username, and profile avatar. This is required if you create a CineWatch account to synchronize your preferences across devices.

Viewing & Playback State

Your watchlist, favorites, rated titles, playback timestamps ("Continue Watching"), audio preference (Sub vs. Dub), and volume levels. When not logged in, this state is maintained solely in your device's browser LocalStorage.

Custom Subtitle Files (.srt / .vtt) — 100% Client-Side

When you use the subtitle synchronization tools or upload a custom subtitle file (e.g. from KurdSubtitle), the file is read and rendered entirely within your browser's local memory. It is never uploaded to, transmitted across, or stored on our servers.

Community Comments

If you post a review or comment on a title, your comment body, chosen username, and avatar are stored and publicly visible to the community.

Privacy-Preserving Analytics (Umami)

We use cookieless, GDPR-compliant analytics via Umami to monitor server uptime, stream performance, and popular titles without tracking individual identities or collecting personal IP addresses.

We do NOT collect credit card numbers, banking details, physical addresses, or government identification.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to operate, maintain, and enhance CineWatch:

  • Providing personalized watchlists, favorites, and automatic resume playback
  • Remembering your subtitle offsets, Kurdish dialogue sync markers, and audio tracks
  • Verifying VIP entitlement tokens to suppress advertisements and grant access to VIP 4K server routes
  • Displaying your profile, username, and community contributions
  • Protecting platform availability against bots, DDoS attacks, and abusive scrapers (via Cloudflare)
We never sell your data

Your personal information is never sold, rented, or traded to third parties or advertising brokers under any circumstances.

4. Account & Security

Account authentication and cloud data storage are handled through Supabase, an enterprise-grade backend infrastructure provider:

  • Passwords are secured with high-entropy bcrypt hashing — plain-text passwords cannot be accessed by anyone, including platform administrators.
  • Active user sessions are authenticated using time-limited JWT tokens.
  • User data is shielded by strict Row Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring each user can only read and write their own data.
  • All browser-to-server communication is strictly enforced with HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption.
Isolated User Data

Your account records, custom lists, and profile settings are strictly isolated and inaccessible to other users or external third parties.

5. Subtitle Engine & Kurdish Tools

CineWatch features an integrated multi-track subtitle engine with advanced Kurdish subtitle synchronization tools, live dialogue explorer (دێڕەکان), timecode offset adjusters, and KurdSubtitle integration:

  • Local Processing: All subtitle file parsing (.srt and .vtt formats) takes place inside your browser’s JavaScript engine. No subtitle texts, dialogue snippets, or user uploads are transferred to or logged on CineWatch servers.
  • Public Community Catalogs: When fetching community Kurdish subtitles via KurdSubtitle or public repositories, requests are routed to public CDN endpoints. No personal identifier is passed with these requests.
  • Offset Preferences: Your customized subtitle timing offsets (e.g., ±1s, ±3s) are remembered in your browser’s local storage solely for playback convenience during your viewing session.

6. Offline Downloads & Referrals

CineWatch provides a dedicated Download Hub (download.html) and stream referral tools for users seeking offline playback:

  • External Endpoint Routing: CineWatch does not host video files on its servers. When you click download or copy a direct media link, you are connected directly to independent third-party file distributors or video hosts.
  • No Transfer Telemetry: We do not log what files you download, how many megabytes you transfer, or your destination IP address when communicating with third-party download servers.

7. VIP & Payment Privacy

Users who choose to upgrade to CineWatch VIP (FastPass) enjoy ad-free viewing, priority 4K streaming server routes, and fast-track download slots:

  • Zero Financial Storage: All subscription transactions, crypto payments, card authorizations, and local payment methods (such as Qi Card, Telegram bots) are handled entirely by specialized third-party payment gateways. CineWatch never sees, collects, or stores your credit card numbers, CVVs, or wallet private keys.
  • Entitlement Tokens: Upon verified payment, your account or browser is issued a secure cryptographic entitlement token (or activation code) that grants ad-free status and 4K server access.

8. Third-Party Services

CineWatch collaborates with reputable third-party infrastructure and content indexing partners:

  • Third-Party Video Streaming Hosts — Video playback is provided by independent third-party embed servers (VidLink, AutoEmbed, 2Embed, Vidsrc, etc.). These hosts operate independently and have their own privacy practices.
  • Supabase — Cloud database, user authentication, and profile sync (privacy policy: supabase.com/privacy).
  • Cloudflare — Content delivery network, DDoS defense, and bot prevention via Cloudflare Turnstile (privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy).
  • Umami Analytics — Cookieless, GDPR-compliant privacy analytics that measures general traffic trends without profiling users.
  • Monetization & Ad Networks — For free tier users, non-intrusive sponsor ads may be delivered by external networks (e.g. Adsterra). These networks may use contextual cookies. VIP members bypass all ad network scripts.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB) — Movie posters, backdrop art, and metadata are served via TMDB public APIs. No user viewing data is shared with TMDB.

9. Cookies & Preferences Center

CineWatch utilizes browser storage (LocalStorage) and lightweight cookies to optimize your playback environment. You have granular control over these categories:

  • Strictly Necessary — Essential session tokens, security verification, and fundamental player controls.
  • Functional & Playback — Video timestamps, volume level, Sub/Dub selection, subtitle sync offsets, and watchlist data.
  • Performance Analytics — Anonymous, cookieless stream quality and page performance metrics via Umami.
  • Advertising (Free Tier Only) — Contextual ad delivery to support free streaming servers. Completely deactivated for VIP subscribers.

10. Official Social Channels

CineWatch maintains an official community presence on Instagram (@cinewatch.official) and other social platforms to share movie trailers, new episode announcements, and platform updates:

  • Third-Party Platform Policies: When you view our reels, follow our page, comment on posts, or send direct messages on Instagram, your activity is governed by Meta’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

11. Your Rights

You have full autonomy over your personal data under global privacy standards (including GDPR and CCPA):

  • Access: Review all personal information linked to your profile from your account settings.
  • Rectification: Update your username, email, or avatar at any time.
  • Erasure: Request permanent deletion of your account and associated history within 30 days via our contact form.
  • Client Data Clearing: Clear your local watchlist, resume points, and subtitle offsets instantly by clearing your browser cache or site data.

12. Children's Privacy

CineWatch is not intended for individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a minor under 13 has provided personal data on CineWatch, please submit an inquiry through our contact form below so we can immediately purge the data.
