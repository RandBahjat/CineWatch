Common questions

How does billing actually work?

Deposit any amount, any time. We deduct bandwidth and storage charges from your balance as you use them. No monthly fees, no subscriptions, no minimums.

What payment methods do you accept?

Bitcoin, USDT, Ethereum, and 30+ other cryptocurrencies via Cryptomus. No credit cards, no PayPal — pure crypto, no questions asked.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. New accounts get a 3-day trial with full platform access and a starter bandwidth allocation — no crypto deposit required.

What counts as bandwidth?

Only successful HTTP 200 and 206 responses on video segments. Failed requests, redirects, and preflight calls never hit your bill.

What is the maximum file size?

Individual uploads are capped at 20 GB per file. Files are chunked, uploaded directly to our transcoding cluster, and processed into adaptive HLS in parallel.

Can I monetize my videos?

Yes. Run VAST preroll and popup ads from any network or your own direct deals. We do not take a cut of your ad revenue.

Can I customize the player with my own branding?

Yes. choose brand colors, customize the play button, and control what shows on the player. Your videos look like yours, not ours.
